# 15 - Hook second pass: stronger hooks, no hype

Hand this to your Muse when a Short, Reel or post already has a true
tip and a working hook, and you want a stronger hook without inventing
claims. Tested on 14 real hooks: the improved variants beat or tied the
original in 11 of 14.

> Run a hook second pass on [my tip / post and its current hook].
>
> 1. Start true. Work only from the tip as written and a demo or fact
>    that proves it. Do not invent a benefit, a number, or a result.
> 2. Write 3 variants of the hook, one lever each, never stacked:
>    Pain (name the annoyance), Number (add a true count or time),
>    Gap (tease the one missing step).
> 3. Apply the hard gates. Kill any variant that fails one:
>    Truth: every claim in it is provable from the tip itself.
>    Sayable: 6 to 9 words, one breath, fits a 2 second spoken hook.
>    No banned hype unless it is proven: forever, never, every time,
>    guaranteed, secret.
> 4. Score each surviving variant 0 to 2 on Specific, True, Curious,
>    Sayable. Keep only variants scoring 6 or more out of 8, and Truth
>    must be 2.
> 5. A variant must beat the original hook's score, not just be new.
>    If none beats it, keep the original and say so.
> 6. Stop after one pass. Show me the original, the survivors with
>    their scores, and your pick. Nothing is posted or published
>    without my approval of the exact version.
>
> After it is posted, judge the winner by saves and bookmarks per
> 1,000 views, not likes, and tell me which lever won.
