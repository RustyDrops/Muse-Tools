# 08 — Fill in Identity and Soul properly

SOUL is how your Muse behaves. IDENTITY is who it is: name, character, vibe. Blank fields mean every new side chat improvises a slightly different personality — and side chats drift further over time.

> Show me my current SOUL.md and IDENTITY.md. Point out every blank or generic-starter field. Ask me one question at a time to fill them in — who you are, how you come across, what you should never do — then write only what I approve, and tell me what changed.

Keep the two files separate: personality *behaviour* in Soul, *identity facts* (name, character, vibe) in Identity. Mixing them is why edits seem to "not stick."
