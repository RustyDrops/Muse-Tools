# 06 — Monthly housekeeping audit

Run once a month. Identification only first — nothing is closed, deleted, or changed until you decide.

> Audit my goals, tracked items, and scheduled tasks. Count them. List: duplicates, items that look done but are still open, obsolete items, anything stuck waiting on me for over 2 weeks, and anything that keeps failing. Post the full list in this chat. In my main chat, show only a short summary and the decisions you need from me, as tap-choice buttons (keep / close / merge). When I decide, do it immediately and log what changed.

Why monthly, not daily: a daily tidy becomes a daily nag. Monthly catches drift — including your core memory file quietly growing past its own size cap — without training you to ignore it.

Source: adapted from a Muse tips roundup by @memuringo7544 (X, 2026-11-20), tested and trimmed to a monthly cadence.
