# Cinematic Video

A gate skill for Muse: no video is generated until a full six-category shooting brief is approved — duration/framing, people, location, movement by the second, camera work, and sound + avoid list — assembled like a small film crew (director, screenplay, cinematography, art direction, sound), with one purpose per shot, subject priority over scenery, and final-frame continuity for follow-ups.

Copy `SKILL.md` into your Muse's custom skills folder and it will walk you through the brief before making anything.
